# 📷 Overview

[🇬🇧 English](📷-Overview) · [🇵🇱 Polski](📷-Przegląd)

**Immich → Google Photos Sync** is a self-hosted Docker service for manually selecting pictures from an Immich album and uploading them to the Google Photos library.

- Immich is the source; Google Photos receives explicitly approved photos.
- Originals remain in Immich.
- No automatic Google Photos album assignment or two-way synchronization.
- A local SQLite database records transfer states.
