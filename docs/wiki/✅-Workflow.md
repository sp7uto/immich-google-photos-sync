# ✅ Select and send

[🇬🇧 English](✅-Workflow) · [🇵🇱 Polski](✅-Wybór-zdjęć)

1. Create an Immich album named `do publikacji`, or change `SOURCE_ALBUM`.
2. Add photos you want to consider for transfer.
3. In the trusted-network web panel, review thumbnails and filenames.
4. Tick the photos you want, then explicitly approve **Send selected**.
5. Follow queue progress. Pause, resume, or cancel between files.
6. Export CSV history or back up SQLite when needed.

Uploads land in the **Google Photos library**, not an automatically selected album. Images are not deleted from Immich.

For uncertain transfers marked `uploading` or `needs_review`, independently verify Google Photos before a manual resolution. Do not blindly retry.
